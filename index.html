Welcome To R.R. Group Of Institutions

R.R. Group Of Institutions is a private engineering institute that was established in 2008. The institution opens a path for all learners to own their future by offering various undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma programs.
With high learning streams such as management and engineering, it is recognized as one of the Best B.Tech, MBA, and Polytechnic Colleges in Lucknow and the Top BTech college in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh.
The institute is well-affirmed by AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education) and affiliated with eminent national bodies such as AKTU (Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Technical University) and the Board of Technical Education.
R.R. group of institutions is allocated as ASSOCHAM (Associated Chambers of Commerce and Industry of India) for the best College award and commended with the ‘Prakriti Ratna Award’ and ‘Shiksha Gaurav Samman’ for its quality education in the field of management and technical education, which counts RRGI among the best engineering colleges in Lucknow.
The admission criteria of the institute are concluded under the passing marks in the entrance exams for the respective courses of your choice, like JEECUP (Joint Entrance Examination Council, Uttar Pradesh) and NATA (National Aptitude Test in Architecture).
