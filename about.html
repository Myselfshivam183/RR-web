About RRGI

A Place For Learning, Discovery, Innovation, Expression and Discourse.


Empowering Future-Ready Professionals through Innovation, Industry Integration & NEP-Aligned Education

R. R. Institute of Modern Technology, Lucknow (RRIMT) was established in 2008 under the Sri Ram Niwas Rukmani Devi Trust, R.R. Group of Institutions (RRGI) has grown into one of the most respected professional institutions in Uttar Pradesh. Located on a vibrant 45+ acre campus in Lucknow, RRGI offers an intellectually stimulating environment designed to nurture learning, research, innovation, and entrepreneurship.

Under the visionary guidance of Chairman Shri Anil Kumar Agarwal and with a team of highly experienced faculty, RRGI is committed to producing value-driven technocrats and business leaders equipped to thrive in a rapidly evolving world.

RRGI stands out for its strong industry-academia collaboration, hosting regular expert talks, industrial visits, live projects, internships, and placement drives in partnership with top companies. Our Innovation & Incubation Centre encourages students to work on real-world problems, startups, and research-based solutions—bridging the gap between academic knowledge and industry needs.

In alignment with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, RRGI has adopted multi-disciplinary learning, skill-based education, flexible curricula, and outcome-based pedagogies to prepare students for global challenges and lifelong success.

Approved by the AICTE, Ministry of Education, Government of India, and affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow, RRGI continues its mission of excellence by integrating academic rigor with ethical values and social responsibility.

At RRGI, we believe in transforming education into a meaningful journey—where knowledge meets purpose, and innovation meets opportunity.

VISION

To achieve excellence in scientific, technical education, and research to create a dynamic and multidisciplinary system to make our students technically and professionally excellent with incorporation of Indian values, morals, and ethics.

MISSION

M1-To provide state of the art facilities, resources, and academic environment which creates competent professionals who can contribute to the development of society, nation, and world.
M2-To establish a perpetual institution-industry interaction to broaden student horizons and strengthen the multidisciplinary approach.
M3-To inculcate skills leading to employability and entrepreneurship with a passion for lifelong learning.
M4-To incorporate a healthy competitive zeal among the students with full of confidence to make them dynamic professionals,full of Indian values, morals, and ethics.
